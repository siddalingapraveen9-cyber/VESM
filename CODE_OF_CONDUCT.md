# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

VESM depends on honest, evidence-based collaboration — `core/GS99.999.md`'s
Professional Integrity principle applies to how contributors treat each other, not
only to how they report engineering evidence. This Code of Conduct states the
minimum standard of behavior expected of everyone participating in the VESM
repository: contributors, maintainers, and anyone commenting on a proposal.

## Expected Behavior

- Engage with the substance of a proposal or critique, not the person making it.
- Disagree openly and specifically — cite the evidence, principle, or criterion in
  `constitution/CHANGE_POLICY.md` a proposal fails to meet, rather than dismissing
  it without reason.
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification before assuming an error was
  intentional.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope, made under `constitution/GOVERNANCE.md`,
  even when you disagree with a specific outcome — raise a new proposal rather than
  circumventing the process.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments directed at any participant.
- Discriminatory language or behavior on the basis of any personal characteristic.
- Deliberately misrepresenting another participant's proposal or position.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Reporting

Concerns about behavior in this repository can be raised by opening an issue marked
as a conduct concern, or, where the concern involves a maintainer, by contacting
another maintainer directly. Reports are handled discreetly and are not used
against the person reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard, and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or other interactions that violate
it. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from
participation in the repository.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all VESM repository spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions) and in any other setting where someone is representing VESM
or its community.
