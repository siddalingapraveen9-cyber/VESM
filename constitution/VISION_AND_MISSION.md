# Vision and Mission

## Mission

Build and maintain a complete, vendor-neutral engineering methodology that enables
individuals, teams, organizations, students, researchers, and AI-assisted developers
to build software systematically, with maximum engineering discipline, using
evidence-driven and verification-based practice in place of guesswork.

The Mission is frozen for the duration of a given VESM version's development, per
`core/VVBM.md`'s Mission Freeze discipline, and is amended only through the process
in `CHANGE_POLICY.md`.

## Vision

A universal engineering methodology that can be applied regardless of programming
language, framework, operating system, AI platform, IDE, vendor, or cloud provider —
usable by a practitioner working with any commercial AI assistant, any local model,
multiple tools in combination, or no AI assistance at all.

VESM's long-term vision is for engineering discipline to be a property of the
process a practitioner follows, not of the specific tools they happen to have
available. A team should be able to adopt VESM today and continue applying it
unchanged as their tools, languages, and AI providers change around them.

## What Success Looks Like

VESM succeeds to the extent that projects built under it exhibit fewer avoidable
failures — failures traceable to unclear objectives, undesigned architecture,
unverified claims, inconsistent quality assessment, or unsafe version control — than
comparable projects built without it, regardless of the technology involved.

VESM does not succeed by being adopted widely for its own sake, by accumulating
features, or by becoming complex. A version of VESM that is smaller, clearer, and
more consistently followed represents more success than one that is larger but
inconsistently applied.

## Boundaries of the Vision

This vision is deliberately bounded. VESM does not aim to become a project
management system, a certification-for-hire business, or a proxy for any single
vendor's product roadmap. Ideas that would expand VESM beyond a vendor-neutral
engineering methodology belong in `ROADMAP.md` for future consideration, not in the
Constitution, and are evaluated against the acceptance criteria in
`CHANGE_POLICY.md` like any other proposed change.
