# VESM v1.1.0

**Release date:** 17 August 2026
**Status:** Active

## Summary

VESM v1.1 strengthens the methodology with explicit evidence, contract, failure-path, freeze, operational, optimization, and scaling controls.

## Major additions

- Evidence-to-Claim Traceability Law
- Contract Boundary Verification Law
- Failure-Path Symmetry Law
- Change-Surface Minimization Law
- Freeze Integrity Law
- Operational Reality Gate
- Verification Depth Scaling Law
- Performance-Without-Regression Rule
- V1/V2 Scale Mode Boundary
- Expanded adversarial verification
- Explicit distinction between mathematical, implementation, adversarial, operational, and release readiness

## Release discipline

VESM v1.1 preserves V1 as the verified reference implementation. Scale Mode begins with V2 only when measured operational evidence identifies a real scaling constraint.

## Verification principle

Every important PASS, FAIL, freeze, release, and readiness claim must trace to concrete evidence.

See `VESM_v1.1.pdf` for the complete VESM v1.1 methodology.

## Version boundary

VESM v1.0.0 remains the previous released version. VESM v1.1.0 is the current active methodology.
